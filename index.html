FAQ

Common questions

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram and Facebook.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes, I work with businesses anywhere. I'm based in Toronto, so for Toronto businesses, in-person content creation can be built right into your Full Service package. If you're outside Toronto and would like me on site, let's talk about what that could look like, with travel quoted separately.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Full Service starts with a four month commitment, which covers your first month of strategy and content plus three months of posting. After that, it's month to month.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. I can build your content around your products, your space and your work. That said, real people tend to perform best, as my retail story shows.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice engagement start to shift within the first month of posting. Real momentum comes from consistency, which is why Full Service starts with four months.
