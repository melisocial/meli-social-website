Meet Shannon

A storyteller at heart, guided by the data.

I love working with small businesses. Owners are so passionate about what they've built and the people they serve, and it's contagious. Helping that passion reach more people is my favourite part of what I do.

My roots are in theatre, where I learned how a good story makes people feel something. Then came a degree in cell biology, where I learned to test, measure and let the evidence decide. Two very different worlds, but together they're a powerful combination. That way of thinking has guided more than a decade of my work in sales and marketing. It's also how I grew the Instagram for the wellness coaching practice I once ran.

Today, I bring that same mix of storytelling and evidence to every business I work with.

Brick + Mortar Retail

An employee-led holiday campaign that lifted organic reach 445%

The store's existing content wasn't generating enough organic reach and engagement, putting its holiday product launch at risk. The goal was to get the holiday launch in front of more people, boost organic engagement and reduce marketing costs. I created a holiday video campaign featuring the store's own employees, and their authentic, relatable energy captured the audience's interest and became the model for future campaigns.

FAQ

Common questions

Which platforms do you manage?

Instagram and Facebook.

Do you work with businesses outside Toronto?

Yes, I work with businesses anywhere. I'm based in Toronto, so for Toronto businesses, in-person content creation can be built right into your Full Service package. If you're outside Toronto and would like me on site, let's talk about what that could look like, with travel quoted separately.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Full Service starts with a four month commitment, which covers your first month of strategy and content plus three months of posting. After that, it's month to month.

Do I need to be on camera?

No. I can build your content around your products, your space and your work. That said, real people tend to perform best, as my retail story shows.

How soon will I see results?

Most clients notice engagement start to shift within the first month of posting. Real momentum comes from consistency, which is why Full Service starts with four months.
